# KODA LIFE

Personal planning application: choose a feasible day, make progress toward goals and projects, reflect without turning personal state into productivity scores. Operate mode throughout the application.

Expo / React Native Web / TypeScript, Supabase, PWA. Current product truth lives in src/features/koda. Today, planner, goals, projects, notes, journal, timer, progress, profile and assistant are all supported. Goal Open Window preserves a central outcome, eight directions and 64 action positions. AI output requires review before application. Goal context and clarification answers persist with the goal. Day SCORE evaluates the originally accepted plan; revisions are separate. Chat, drafts, offline storage, synchronization, record ownership and IDs must survive visual changes.

No teams, fictional analytics, mandatory daily routine or invented progress. Native controls, keyboard access and readable Russian text matter more than ornament. Redesign must remain local until explicitly authorized for publication.

KODA LIFE 4.0 opens as a live Widget Desktop. Existing Today remains a full day-planning section; habits now have an explicit full section with daily/weekday schedules and pauses. Goal milestones are the only roadmap stage entity. Widget layout is local per account and device; domain records continue through existing Supabase app-state sync.
